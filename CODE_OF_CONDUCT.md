# 社区行为规范

保持尊重、围绕技术问题讨论，不骚扰、歧视或披露他人隐私。不要分享未获授权的音频资源、账号数据或个人备份。维护者可移除不符合这些要求的内容，并限制重复违规者参与。

## English

Be respectful and keep discussions technical. Harassment, discrimination and disclosure of private information are not welcome. Do not share unauthorized audio, account data or personal backups. Maintainers may remove violating content and restrict repeat offenders.
